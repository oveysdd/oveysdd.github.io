Topology Optimization for Weight Reduction

Topology optimization is a computational design method that determines the optimal material distribution within a given design space. It is one of the most powerful tools in modern structural design.

The SIMP Method

Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization (SIMP) is the most widely used density-based topology optimization method. It assigns a density variable to each element and penalizes intermediate densities to push the solution toward a binary (solid/void) design.

Optimization Problem Formulation

The standard compliance minimization problem is formulated as:

Minimize:    C(x) = U^T K U
Subject to:  V(x) / V0 = volfrac
              0 <= x_e <= 1

Where x is the vector of element densities, K is the global stiffness matrix, U is the displacement vector, and volfrac is the prescribed volume fraction.

Practical Considerations

  • Filtering: Prevents checkerboard patterns and mesh dependency
  • Projection: Improves boundary definition
  • Manufacturing constraints: Ensure the design is producible
  • Post-processing: Convert the density field into a CAD model

Conclusion

Topology optimization enables designs that are lighter, stronger, and more efficient than intuition-based approaches. Combined with additive manufacturing, it opens new possibilities in engineering design.
